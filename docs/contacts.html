Town Clerk Directory

Charter boundary sections almost never describe a boundary — they cite a plat recorded in a town's land records. These are the offices that hold those records, and the people to ask for them.

Why this is here. See caveat 6 — for the 10 legislatively chartered districts, the Title 24 charter points to a book and page in a town clerk's office rather than giving geometry. Cold Brook Fire District No. 1, for example, is defined as "recorded in Book 111, page 184 … of the land records of the Town of Wilmington." Pulling that plat starts with the clerk.
